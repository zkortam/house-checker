## Point of sale (seller customarily)
3R $379 (DBI Table 1A-L, 2026-07-12); water conservation cert every sale (≤1.6 gpf toilets, ≤2.5 gpm showers, ≤2.2 aerators, leak test); RECO only for pre-7/1/1978 buildings without recorded compliance; smoke/CO and water-heater bracing statements; SFPUC flood map disclosure. ## Insurance
