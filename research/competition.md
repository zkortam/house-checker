- "None acts on it" is FALSE (disclosure AIs draft requests/offers). Narrow claim holds: nobody joins listing claims × assessor × permits × enforcement.
- Concept is priced elsewhere (Gryd lender flag for unpermitted improvements; assessor aerial vs sketch) → problem is real.
- No Verisk "unpermitted work score" exists.
- Redfin notes listing is usually more current than assessor → flags = "discrepancy to resolve", not "unpermitted".
- Defensible: SF-specific joins (units vs 3R authorized occupancy; baths vs plumbing permits; "renovated" vs unfinaled permits; soft-story tier), low false alarms, cited flags, distribution via SF buyer agents.
- Copyable: permit lists, risk scores, overlays, drafted letters.
- 3R fee conflicting: $379 (sf.gov) vs $148–171 (older pages).

Safer phrasing: "Today's tools either show city records or summarize seller documents. House Checker is the first we know of that tests the listing's own claims against San Francisco's records, cites each mismatch, and drafts the next step."
