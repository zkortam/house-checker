# SF buying process & legal — condensed (research agent, 2026-11-20). Not legal advice.

## Timeline / where value lands
- Listings live ~Fri, offer date ~7–14 days later; disclosure package (100–200 pp: TDS, SPQ, AVID, 3R, permits, soft-story, NHD, prelim title, pre-inspections) 5–14 days before offers.
- 2026: preemptives within ~3 days, "no contingencies" common; 1 in 3 sales all-cash (Jan–Sep); 144 homes ≥$1M over asking H1. Buyer pre-inspection $400–$900. (sfstandard.com 2026-07-23, 2026-07-07, 2026-09-20)
- Value moments: (1) Day-0 triage — "is this worth a pre-inspection?" (2) disclosure-package reconciliation (3) inspector checklist (4) offer-day risk pricing. Trust/probate/estate sales exempt from TDS (Civ. Code §1102.2) → records matter most there.

## Existing docs vs tool
- 3R (Housing Code §351(a)): authorized occupancy/use, building permits ONLY ("no history of any plumbing or electrical permits"), abatement status, soft-story. $379 from 2026-07-12. Non-owners can order (6/day). sf.gov/request3r
- NHD (Civ. Code §1103): flood, dam, fire, fault, liquefaction/landslide — seller pays $50–140 → hazards are DUPLICATE. Safe harbor §1102.4.
- New value: electrical/plumbing permits, final status vs advertised renovations, full complaint/NOV history incl. closed, dollar framing, questions/records request/inspector checklist.

## Consequences (dollar framing)
- 30k–50k illegal units in SF (SF Planning).
- Legalization (Planning Code §207.3): avg ~$60k (2017–18); $25k–120k garage conversions; extreme $750k–$1M (Chronicle Cole Valley). SF ceiling 7'6" vs state 7'0" blocks many.
- AB 2533 (Gov. Code §66311.7, 2025): amnesty for pre-2020 unpermitted ADUs unless substandard.
- Removal restricted: Planning Code §317 — Conditional Use usually required; exceptions (c)(4),(c)(7),(c)(10).
- Rent control: illegal in-law makes SFH a non-exempt 2-unit building. Relocation (3/1/26–2/28/27): $8,245/tenant, max $24,733/unit, +$5,497 senior/disabled/child household (Rent Board form 579).
- Lending: Fannie B4-1.3-05 — non-compliant ADU eligible only under narrow conditions; UAD 3.6 mandatory 2026-11-02 with ADU section.
- Insurance: knob-and-tube → cancellations/FAIR Plan; replacement can exceed $100k.
- Seller liability: as-is ≠ fraud shield; CCP §338(d) 3 years from discovery (Vergara v. Pan Partnership 2018) → a flag starts the clock; keep dated reports.

## Legal lines
- B&P §10131: negotiating for compensation = broker. DRE AI advisory 2026-03-17: licensee responsible; flagging/summarizing OK; not attorney advice. → Don't call it "buyer's agent", don't negotiate.
- UPL B&P §6125: don't draft contract clauses → "issues to raise with your agent", reference standard C.A.R. forms.
- Fair housing: drop crime, 311 quality-of-life, schools (CA FEHA disparate impact, 2 CCR §12155).
- RESPA §8 / B&P §7197: no inspector referral fees; disclose any paid placement; buyer pays inspector directly.
- Labels: "Records differ from listing", "Not found in City records", "Unverifiable" — never "illegal"/"misrepresented".
- Records request: notarized DBI owner affidavit for plans (H&S §19851) + finaled job cards.
- Disclaimer: "Compares the listing's claims with public City records and flags differences for you and your agent to investigate. It is not an inspection, appraisal, or legal advice. Records may be incomplete, and unpermitted work may not appear in any record."

## Who pays
1. Buyer's agents/brokerages (AB 2992 written service agreements, 3-month cap; ~2.4% commission) — licensed partner absorbs DRE/UPL risk.
2. Buyers $50–150/property (vs one more $400–900 pre-inspection).
3. Lenders/AMCs, insurers later; investors / 2–4 unit premium tier.
