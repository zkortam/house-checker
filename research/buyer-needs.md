# What SF buyers need to know — condensed (research agent, 2026-11-20). Reddit blocked; used Blind, BiggerPockets, SocketSite, news, city docs.

## Headline surprises (with sources)
- Illegal unit MERGER: 524 Vallejo, $4.75M (2021), 4 units merged w/o approval; Supervisors rejected legalization Apr 2026; owner liable regardless of knowledge (sfist.com 2026-03-09, 2026-04-09). §317 Conditional Use for any unit removal.
- Illegal in-law w/ tenant: 30–50k illegal units; rent control/eviction rules still apply.
- 3R omits plumbing/electrical; can overstate units (DBI IS G-27).
- DBI Orders of Abatement recorded against title; Planning violation $250/day, current owner responsible.
- Knob-and-tube → non-renewals (Safeco pre-1976, Farmers pre-1925 multi-unit, State Farm); SF FAIR Plan homes 364 (2019) → 823 (Axios 2025-01-14).
- Foundation (brick rubble) $80k–$300k+ (low-reliability source).
- Sewer lateral: owner owns upper lateral; repair $8–10k, replace $20k+; DBI lateral permit ~$285.
- Sq ft: appraisals 22% below advertised happen.
- Condo: median special assessment record $1,100 (2025); HOA insurance +75% 2021–25; ~730 CA complexes on Fannie "unavailable" list, 21 in SF (lender-only; Chronicle 2025-03).
- Property-line windows not protected (280 Union "spite wall").
- Neighbor construction damage (1990 Folsom; illegal hillside excavation $1M+ judgment).
- Owner move-in: 1 unit/building, ≥25% owner, 36 months occupancy, relocation, 5-yr re-rent cap.
- Sidewalks/trees: city responsible since Prop E (Jul 2017) for tree damage; owner liable for pre-7/2017 and non-tree damage; 31k-site backlog.

## Tedious today
Disclosure packets 100–200 pp (TIC up to 300); 3R $379; plumbing/electrical separate searches; expediters $350–800 simple, $15k+ complex; Rent Board buyout data partly office-only, annual PDFs; eviction notices on DataSF since 1997; Planning "Permits in My Neighborhood"; HOA doc stacks. Competitor: HomeClick (CA AI packet red flags with $ estimates).
Costs: general inspection $500–900; pest $150–300; sewer scope avg ~$1,373; structural engineer ~$500–1.5k (est.); attorney ~$400–700/hr (est.).

## Condo/TIC
HOA resale package (Civ. Code §5300 budget, §5550 reserve study incl. SB 326 balconies), minutes for assessments under discussion (>5% budget needs vote), litigation, insurance. FHA list public (HUD); Fannie unavailable list lender-only → ask lender for project check pre-offer. Rental caps ≥25% (Civ. Code §4741). TIC: fractional loans +0.25–1.25%, 10–20% below condos; condo conversion lottery, ~8-yr no-fault eviction bar; Expedited Conversion for 2-unit resumed Apr 2024. BMR resale caps for life.

## Upside
ADU: state program ministerial vs local program (rent control may attach). SB 9: lot ≥2,400 sf, no rent-controlled units, no Ellis in 15 yrs, no tenant in 3 yrs, owner 3-yr affidavit; ~32,700 eligible RH-1 parcels. Family Zoning Plan adopted Dec 2025 (taller on corridors; upside for you, risk next door). SB 79 local plan → no default SB 79 until ~2032. Historic Category A/B/C, Article 10/11. NSRs run with title.

## Neighborhood (safe): Planning/DBI projects nearby, §311 notices (150 ft, 30 days), SFPUC flood map, CGS zones, trees/sidewalk, RPP, transit, freeway/rail/airport noise, Mello-Roos districts (Mission Bay, Transbay, Hunters Point). AVOID: crime ratings, school quality framed demographically, demographic or lifestyle language.
